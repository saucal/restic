## #22081 — reply (keep open, his call)

> Measured. Bytes allocated to verify one blob, `DecodeAll` vs a streaming decoder:
>
> | blob | `DecodeAll` | streaming |
> |---|---|---|
> | 64 KiB | 74 KB | 262 KB |
> | 1 MiB | 1.06 MB | 2.49 MB |
> | 8 MiB | 8.4 MB | 9.9 MB |
> | 16 MiB | 17 MB | 10 MB |
> | 64 MiB | 67 MB | 9.9 MB |
> | 256 MiB | 268 MB | 9.8 MB |
>
> Up to 8 MiB the streaming decoder allocates a window as large as the blob plus its own state, so a single streaming path would make every data blob more expensive. Above that its memory stays flat. Speed is similar at all sizes.
>
> Only very wide trees produce blobs that large, though, so with chunked trees this stops mattering. If a second path isn't worth it until then, I'm fine with closing this. As mentioned, I'm not a Go expert; this is what worked for us. If there's an issue or branch for the format v3 work where help would be useful, please point me to it.
